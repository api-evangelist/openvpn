CloudConnexa and NordLayer both promise to replace legacy VPN sprawl with a single, cloud-delivered platform for secure access — but they start from different architectural assumptions. CloudConnexa unifies ZTNA, secure internet access, and site-to-site connectivity into one multi-tenant overlay network spanning all its global PoPs, with no per-feature tier gating. NordLayer, built on the Nord Security stack, takes a gateway-based approach instead — routing devices through shared or dedicated cloud gateways over its WireGuard-based NordLynx protocol, with many of its advanced controls reserved
