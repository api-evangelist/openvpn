CloudConnexa and Check Point Harmony SASE both promise to replace a stack of point solutions with a single, cloud-delivered service — but they draw the line between “included” and “add-on” in very different places. This breakdown covers architecture, Zero Trust controls, the security stack, and where each pricing model works best, so you can match the right platform to your environment.
