Luis Tarango, Director of Engineering (Director de Ingeniería) at TGC, joins the "Front Lines of Security" panel at OpenVPN [Con]nected 2026, our free virtual event on October 22, 2026. TGC is an OpenVPN partner with offices in the United States and Mexico that helps public- and private-sector organizations put the right technology in place, even when they have limited technical resources.
