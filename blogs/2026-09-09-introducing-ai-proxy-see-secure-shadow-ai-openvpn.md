The short answer: AI Proxy is a new CloudConnexa capability that shows admins which employees are sending what to which AI services — ChatGPT, Claude, and others — and lets them set policy, block risky prompts, cap usage, and generate compliance evidence, without installing a separate agent. It's moving from internal testing into Beta. Take the two-minute AI Proxy Beta interest survey to help shape it and get notified the moment it opens.
