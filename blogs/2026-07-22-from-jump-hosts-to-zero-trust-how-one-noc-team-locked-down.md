Enterprise engineering teams don't outgrow their VPN overnight. It happens gradually, a jump host here, a manually issued key there, until one day the network operations center (NOC) is spending more time managing access than managing infrastructure. That's exactly where Shashikanth Aitha, Senior Network Operations Engineer at Accolite Digital, and his team found themselves before deploying OpenVPN Access Server through AWS Marketplace .
