The US CLOUD Act lets US law enforcement compel a US-headquartered company to produce data it controls, regardless of where that data is physically stored. It doesn't matter if the servers sit in Frankfurt, Paris, or Dublin. What matters is which company controls the system — and what country's courts that company answers to.
